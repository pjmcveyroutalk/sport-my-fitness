About this notice

This notice covers information handled through the Sport My Fitness website. Sport My Fitness is the Milwaukee fitness business presented on this site by Yoshi Barnes. Questions about this notice can be sent using the contact details below.

Information shared during training, in person or through a separate service may require a separate notice. This website notice does not describe an active online payment or customer-account system.

Information you provide

The inquiry form asks for your name, email address, training interest, and goals or questions. Your message may also include other information you choose to provide. Emailing or calling us shares the information you include in that communication.

Keep website messages general. Please do not submit medical records, detailed health information, financial account information, passwords or information about a child through the inquiry form. Contact Yoshi to discuss an appropriate way to share information needed for training.

How information is used

Inquiry information is used to respond to questions, discuss services you have requested, and maintain the related correspondence. Website request information may also be processed to deliver pages, troubleshoot errors and protect the service.

Sending an inquiry is not a newsletter subscription. The current website has no newsletter enrollment or advertising-pixel feature.

Services used by this website

  • FormSubmit and email: the inquiry form sends its fields directly to FormSubmit for delivery to the business email address, Yoshi_barnes@outlook.com. FormSubmit and the email provider process the message as part of that service. FormSubmit uses spam filtering and Google reCAPTCHA to help distinguish people from automated submissions; those checks may process technical information about your browser and request. Delivery is subject to the provider's setup and availability. See FormSubmit and its privacy information.
  • Vercel: website hosting and delivery may process request information such as your IP address, browser details, page requested and request time. See Vercel’s privacy notice.
  • Google Maps: the Contact page includes an embedded map. Loading or interacting with it can send technical information to Google and may involve Google cookies, subject to your browser and Google settings. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • External destinations: social, media and other external links take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Information may also be disclosed where applicable law requires it or where reasonably necessary to address security incidents, protect legal rights or respond to lawful requests.

Cookies and browser storage

The website saves your light or dark appearance choice in local browser storage. This preference remains until you change it or clear the site’s stored data. The private review area uses an essential sign-in cookie that expires after eight hours or is removed on logout.

Google Analytics and advertising pixels are not currently integrated into this website. Third-party services, including the embedded map and hosting provider, may still process information under their own policies.

You can manage cookies and site storage through your browser. Blocking them may affect sign-in, saved preferences or embedded features. Any future analytics or advertising setup will require a fresh privacy and consent review before activation.

Retention and security

Inquiry messages become correspondence handled through the delivery service and business email account. The current website does not maintain its own customer-message database. Provider copies and logs are subject to the relevant provider’s practices.

Sport My Fitness will retain correspondence only for the period needed to handle the inquiry and related business, security or legal obligations. Exact retention procedures are being confirmed before launch. No website or email system can promise absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can choose to contact us by phone instead of submitting the form. You may ask about information you have provided, request a correction, or request deletion by emailing the address below. Please provide enough context to identify the correspondence without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Requests will be handled under applicable law, with reasonable identity verification where needed. Some records may need to be retained for legal or legitimate business purposes. This notice does not assume that every jurisdiction’s privacy law applies to every visitor.

Children’s information

Website inquiries are intended to be submitted by adults. Children under 13 should not submit personal information through this website. If you believe a child’s information has been submitted, contact us so we can review the submission and take appropriate steps.

Updates and contact

This notice will be updated when website practices change. A revised date will identify the latest version. The business identity, provider configuration and retention procedures must be confirmed before this review draft becomes the public notice.
