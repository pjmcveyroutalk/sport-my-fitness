Current shop status

The website currently displays apparel design samples and a book preview. It does not accept online merchandise orders or take payments. A product preview or inquiry is not an order confirmation.

This page concerns merchandise offered through this website. For an existing purchase made directly with Sport My Fitness, contact Yoshi and refer to the terms supplied for that purchase.

Before online orders open

Before customers can place an order, the relevant product page and checkout will identify:

  • The actual product, available sizes or formats, price and availability.
  • Shipping destinations, processing estimates, shipping charges and any applicable taxes.
  • Expected shipment timing, including any clearly identified preorder arrangement.
  • Return and exchange eligibility, the request period, item-condition requirements and responsibility for return shipping.
  • Any product-specific limitations and how to contact the business about an order.

No return window, shipping price, delivery guarantee or “final sale” restriction is established by this review draft.

Shipment timing and delays

For future covered online merchandise orders, shipment promises must have a reasonable basis. If no shipment time is stated, applicable federal rules generally require a reasonable basis to ship within 30 days of a properly completed order.

If a covered order cannot ship on time, Sport My Fitness must follow applicable delay-notice requirements, obtain consent where required, or cancel and provide the required refund for unshipped merchandise. This draft does not replace those legal obligations with a blanket no-refund rule.

Incorrect, damaged or missing items

For a merchandise problem, contact Sport My Fitness with the order reference, a description of the issue and relevant photographs if available. Please do not send payment-card information or return an item without first obtaining the return instructions.

The business will review the issue and explain the available resolution under the purchase terms and applicable law. This page does not limit any consumer right or remedy that cannot legally be limited.

Digital editions

A digital edition of Yoshi’s book is being prepared, but it is not available for purchase through this website. Its delivery method, permitted use, access requirements and refund terms must be provided before digital checkout is enabled.

The physical-merchandise shipping discussion above does not describe delivery of a digital file.
