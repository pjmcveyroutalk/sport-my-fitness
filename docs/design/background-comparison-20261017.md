## Diagnosis and decision

The preceding runtime forced every hero to the same minimum height. At 390px,
Programs had about 237px after its supporting line; Contact also had a substantial
unused band after its line and credit. Matching overall height was the wrong
uniformity constraint for short mobile copy.

Keep shared headline size, origin, line height, buttons and photo treatment.
Below 801px, let each inner hero end after its actual copy with a 32px breathing
space. Keep Home's approved photographic composition and desktop hero geometry.
The browser regression check now measures the remaining band and the transition
into the next section instead of requiring equal mobile hero heights.

For the background, replace the dense fine diagonal repeat with broad charcoal
planes, a restrained edge highlight and turquoise directional light. Keep the
existing grain as a finishing layer. Apply the shared treatment to content
sections and the footer so the design continues beyond the hero. No extra
animation, generated pictures, icons, text or duplicate actions are added.
Keep the angled planes behind the hero photography, not as stripes over people.

PJ also requested removal of the redundant landing-page link group in the later
screenshot. The entire “Find your training” section and its three service links
are removed. The main menu and two useful hero actions remain; full service
information remains on the existing Training and Programs pages.

The exact original logo, all source photographs, original motion scripts, menu
behavior, rates, credits and remaining substantive copy and destinations are preserved.
